# Bundled ROMs

These are the **MEGA65 Open ROMs** (https://github.com/MEGA65/open-roms),
clean-room replacements for the Commodore 64 KERNAL, BASIC and character
ROMs, licensed LGPL-3.0-or-later (some BASIC parts MIT, Microsoft). They are
**not** Commodore's copyrighted ROMs.

| basic_generic.rom | bin/basic_generic.rom | 6f0dec43fad06b22d2dbdbc6f2146627 |
| chargen_openroms.rom | bin/chargen_openroms.rom | 7d7ad88e87a36ea33c18c8bd74153397 |

To replace them in the app, open Settings > Emulator ROMs and pick your own
ROM files. They are stored only on your device.
